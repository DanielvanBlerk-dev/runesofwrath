The Tournament

Every century, when the old runes awaken, a single tournament is called. No arena is neutral, no rule is sacred, and no warrior is welcome to leave.

Eight fighters, each bound to a rune and a grudge, are drawn from the corners of a broken world. The runes lend them terrible power — and take something in return.

Only one will carry the wrath home.
